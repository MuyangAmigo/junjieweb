# Business Analytics Workflow — From Data to Deck

A practical five-step workflow for doing business analytics work, from high-level trends all the way to a polished narrative. Good reference for structuring any data project.

## Step 1: Look at High-Level Business Trends

Start with the big picture — understand where the business stands before diving into details.

![sales dashboard regional performance](https://junjieblob.blob.core.windows.net/images/sales-dashboard-regional-performance.png)

## Step 2: SQL Querying to Pull Raw Data

Get into the database and extract the data you need. BigQuery is a solid tool for this at scale.

![bigquery query editor](https://junjieblob.blob.core.windows.net/images/bigquery-query-editor.png)

## Step 3: Import to Google Sheets + Data Wrangling

Bring the raw data into Sheets and clean it up. "Data wrangling" here means VLOOKUPs, pivot tables, and any transformations needed to make the data usable.

![google sheets country population query](https://junjieblob.blob.core.windows.net/images/google-sheets-country-population-query.png)

## Step 4: Visualize in Google Sheets

Turn the wrangled data into charts and graphs. Keep it readable — the goal is clarity, not visual complexity.

![public data dashboard](https://junjieblob.blob.core.windows.net/images/public-data-dashboard.png)

## Step 5: Develop Business Insights and Narrative in a Deck

This is where the real value gets created. The charts alone don't tell the story — you need to interpret them, surface the "so what," and present a clear narrative.

![global payments revenue growth chart](https://junjieblob.blob.core.windows.net/images/global-payments-revenue-growth-chart.png)

The deck is the deliverable. Everything above is just how you get there.
