# Project Management Workflow — Thinking Through One Thing End-to-End

A structured framework for thinking through any project or initiative from start to finish. More comprehensive than most PM templates.

![abstract data visualization wall](https://junjieblob.blob.core.windows.net/images/abstract-data-visualization-wall.png)

## The Framework

**0. Requirements (需求)**
Start by defining what you're actually being asked to do.

**A. What am I building? (项目/产品定义)**
Define the project or product clearly before anything else.

**B. Is it worth doing? (可行性评估/SWOT)**
Run a feasibility check. Is there a real reason to do this? What are the risks and opportunities?

**C. What do I want to get out of it? (目标)**
Set clear goals — what does success look like?

**D. What resources do I need? (资源评估)**
Inventory: time, people, money, tools.

**E. How do I get those resources — and what if I can't?**
Identify channels for resource acquisition. Have a backup plan for constraints.

**F. How do I allocate resources? (时间/人力/金钱)**
Prioritize and distribute across the dimensions that matter.

**G0. How do I actually get it done? (5W)**
Which parts are most critical? What's urgent vs. important? Build a priority order.

**G1. What's the worst case I can accept? (下限)**
Define the floor. What would be a failure, and how do you prevent it?

**G2. How do I make this exceptional? (上限)**
Define the ceiling. What would make this great, not just good?

**G3. Risk management**
- What risks exist?
- How do you prevent them?
- If something unexpected happens, how do you respond?

**G4. What data do I need to collect? (埋点)**
Define measurement and tracking requirements up front, not as an afterthought.

**H. Archive and retrospective (归档/复盘)**
Document the process and outcomes. Review what worked, what didn't, what you'd change.
