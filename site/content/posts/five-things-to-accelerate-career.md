# 5 Things That Actually Accelerate Your Career

> **TLDR:** Know the people, ask smart questions, escalate properly, run tight meetings, and make your manager look good. That's basically it.

Reference: [Jeff Su on YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=j9YeEQR8nKQ&ab_channel=JeffSu)

## 1. Know Who Does What

Find the right people *before* you need them. Proactively schedule 1:1s and use them well:
- Understand their KPIs and OKRs — find alignment
- Learn their personal goals and what drives them
- Ask what resources they have access to
- Always end with: "Who else should I be talking to?"

## 2. Know How to Ask for Help

Don't just show up empty-handed. When you need help:
- Show what you've already tried
- Focus your ask on topics tied to your core KPIs

Makes a huge difference in whether people actually want to help you.

## 3. Know When to Escalate

Don't let things fester. If something's going sideways, loop in your manager early. The goal isn't to dump problems — it's to keep them informed so they can support you.

## 4. Know How to Run a Productive Meeting

Meetings are where you build (or lose) trust and autonomy.
- Align with stakeholders individually *before* the meeting
- Only present what's relevant to the people in the room
- Always send a recap email with clear action items and owners

The payoff: people trust you with more freedom and fewer check-ins.

## 5. Know Your Boss

Your manager can only advocate for you if you give them the tools to do it:
- Regularly document your wins
- Build a "treasure trove" of achievements with real metrics
- Make it easy for them to say good things about you when it counts
