# Senior Talent Management — Tencent's Evaluation Framework

Notes from Tencent's HR material on how they assess senior talent. Interesting framework to internalize whether you're being evaluated or evaluating others.

Source: 腾讯人力资源管理 · 第七章 高级人才管理

## The Two-Axis Model

Tencent uses a matrix with **4 horizontal dimensions** and **7 vertical dimensions**:

### Horizontal Dimensions (管理层面)
- 管理自己 — Managing yourself
- 管理工作 — Managing work
- 管理团队 — Managing the team
- 管理战略与变革 — Managing strategy and change

### Vertical Dimensions (核心素质)
- 正直诚信 — Integrity and honesty
- 激情 — Passion
- 团队管理与人才培养 — Team management and talent development
- 全局观 — Big-picture thinking
- 前瞻变革 — Foresight and adaptability
- 专业决策 — Professional decision-making
- 关注用户体验 — User experience focus

The higher you go, the more the vertical dimensions (especially 全局观 and 前瞻变革) start to matter. At the senior level it's less about execution and more about vision and growing people around you.
