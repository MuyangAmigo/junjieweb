# What "Doing Work Thoroughly" Actually Means

A framework for how to communicate at different levels of an organization. Simple but surprisingly underrated.

## The Seven Work Communication Principles

**汇报工作说结果 — Report results, not process**
- Lead with outcomes. Your boss wants the result first.
- 结果思维 is the primary mode of thinking at work.

**布置工作说标准 — When delegating, define the standard**
- Set clear benchmarks and norms.
- This defines the scope of work and prevents ambiguity.

**请示工作说方案 — When asking for direction, bring options**
- Don't make your boss answer open-ended questions.
- Give them a multiple-choice, not a blank canvas.

**总结工作说流程 — When reviewing, walk through the process**
- Identify what worked, what went wrong, and what to learn from it.

**交接工作讲道德 — When handing off, be thorough and honest**
- No withholding information, no obstacles.
- Clean, categorized, complete handoff.

**回忆工作说感受 — When reflecting on work, ask yourself**
- What was I actually doing?
- How much value did it create?
- Could someone else have done it?
- Why was I so busy?

**关心下级问过程 — When checking in with your team, focus on the process**
- Listen actively, care about the details.

## Bonus: The Three-Level Structure

| Level | Focus |
| --- | --- |
| 高层 (Senior Leaders) | 做什么 — What to do (Decision-makers) |
| 中层 (Middle Management) | 如何做 — How to do it (Managers) |
| 基层 (Frontline) | 何时做 — When to do it (Executors) |

The mistake a lot of people make is operating at the wrong level — frontline trying to make strategy, or senior leaders micromanaging execution.
