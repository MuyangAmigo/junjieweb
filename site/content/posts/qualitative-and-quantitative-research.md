# Qualitative vs. Quantitative Research — Quick Reference

A simple cheat sheet for remembering the difference between the two. Comes up all the time in product and UX work.

## Quantitative Research (定量研究)

- Purpose: **Test** hypotheses
- Output: Expressed in **numbers**
- Scale: Larger sample sizes
- Analysis method: Math or statistical analysis

## Qualitative Research (定性研究)

- Purpose: **Formulate** hypotheses
- Output: Expressed in **words**
- Scale: Smaller sample sizes
- Analysis method: Summarize, categorize, interpret

## When to Use Which

Think of it this way: qualitative helps you figure out *what* questions to ask, while quantitative helps you *answer* those questions at scale. In practice, the best research uses both — start with qual to understand the problem, then validate with quant.
