# PREP — A Framework for Logical Expression

A simple but effective framework for structuring arguments and responses clearly. Super useful for interviews, presentations, or anytime you need to make a point convincingly.

## The PREP Framework

| Step | What It Means |
| --- | --- |
| **P** — Point | State your conclusion or main argument upfront (结论和观点) |
| **R** — Reason | Explain why — the reasoning behind your point (原因和理由) |
| **E** — Example | Back it up with a concrete example (实例举证) |
| **P** — Point | Reinforce your conclusion at the end (强调观点) |

The key move is leading with your conclusion, not burying it at the end. Makes you sound more decisive and easier to follow.
