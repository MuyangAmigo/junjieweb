# Product Management Is NOT About What You Think

> **TLDR:** PM work isn't about writing specs and running meetings. It's about deeply understanding problems, aligning everyone toward a shared goal, and creating products that customers genuinely love — while also making business sense.

A note I saved that resonated a lot. The author has 9 years of PM experience and this is their honest take.

## What PM Is NOT About

People often think the job is:

- Asking customers about requirements
- Writing detailed specifications
- Creating prototypes and wireframes
- Assigning tasks to developers
- Verifying and accepting others' work
- Obsessing over velocity, deadlines, and roadmaps
- Mastering Scrum to perfection
- Acting like the CEO of the product

Anyone can do that stuff.

## What PM Is Actually About

- Understanding customer problems, needs, and desires (深层次的)
- Understanding the market and business in depth
- Collaborating closely with engineers and designers
- Identifying opportunities, finding solutions, tackling risks — together
- Marrying customer goals with business goals
- Influencing others toward a common goal
- Being humble (it's okay to not be the smartest person in the room)
- Experimenting to validate assumptions
- Leading without authority
- Turning chaos into clarity

## The Questions Worth Starting With

Before building anything, ask:

- Why are we building this?
- Why now?
- For whom?
- What's the unique value?
- How does it align with company vision and business strategy?
- What does success look like — and how do we measure it?
- What are the customer's functional, emotional, and social needs?
- How does it create business value?
- Can we buy it instead of building it?
- Will customers know how to use it?
- Is it legally and financially feasible?
- How do we bring it to market? Do we have the channels?
- Should we even do it? Any ethical considerations?
- What are the riskiest assumptions? How do we validate them?
- What does the data say?
- How can we maximize learning with minimum effort?
- What else can go wrong?

Be curious. Keep learning. Question solutions handed down to you.

> "Product management is about creating a product customers love, yet also works for our business." — Marty Cagan, *Inspired*
