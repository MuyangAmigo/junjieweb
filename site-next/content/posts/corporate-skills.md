# Corporate Skills — Communication Frameworks That Actually Work

A collection of frameworks for more effective workplace communication. Less about soft skills fluff, more about concrete techniques.

![corporate skills presentation icons](/images/corporate-skills-presentation-icons.png)

## Storytelling

Good storytelling at work isn't about pretty visuals or being a charismatic speaker. It's about one thing: **convincing your audience that you're right**.

## The SCQA Framework

Designed to influence others' actions. Use it whenever you need to make a persuasive case.

**SCQA = Situation → Complication → Question → Answer**

**Example: Asking for an early promotion in a 1:1**
- **Situation** — I'd like to be considered for an early promotion.
- **Complication** — I know there's a limited number of slots and I'm not due until next year.
- **Question** — What can we do between now and then for you to feel comfortable nominating me a cycle early?
- **Answer** — Here's a list of my achievements with quantifiable metrics. I'd love a candid discussion.

Notice how it's not aggressive — it frames the problem collaboratively and leads with a solution.

## The BLUF Technique

**Bottom Line Up Front** — put your conclusion first, details second.

Applies to:
- Emails
- Reports
- Presentations

Stop burying the lead. Most people lose their audience in the preamble. State the bottom line, then support it.

## Process Optimization

Simple three-step loop for improving anything:
1. **Identify a problem**
2. **Implement an efficient solution**
3. **Build a reliable system** (so it stays fixed)

The third step is the one people skip — they fix the problem but don't build the system that prevents it from recurring.
