# SMART Goal Setting

The classic framework for setting goals that actually stick. Simple, but surprisingly easy to skip steps on.

## The Five Criteria

| Letter | Meaning | 中文 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **S** | Specific | 具体的 |
| **M** | Measurable | 可度量的 |
| **A** | Attainable | 可实现的 |
| **R** | Relevant | 有相关性的 |
| **T** | Time-bound | 有时限的 |

![smart goals study plan table](/images/smart-goals-study-plan-table.jpeg)

## Why This Matters

Goals without these criteria tend to stay vague — "get better at X" vs. "complete Y course by March 31st and apply it in Z project." The second version actually tells you when you've succeeded. The first doesn't.

Relevant is the one people skip most often. A goal can be specific, measurable, and time-bound but still be working toward the wrong thing. Always check that it actually connects to a larger objective.
