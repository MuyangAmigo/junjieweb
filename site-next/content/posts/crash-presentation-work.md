# Crash Course: Presenting at Work

Five quick tips for better work presentations. These apply whether you're presenting to your team or to leadership.

## Tip 1: Humor Through Relatability

If you're going to be funny, make sure the joke lands by being relevant to who's in the room:

- **Universal** — Things everyone can relate to (current events, shared experiences)
- **Company-wide** — Things all employees can relate to (performance reviews, all-hands stress)
- **Specific** — Inside jokes that only a few people get (risky — use sparingly)

## Tip 2: Keep Your Slides Clean

> "Simplicity is the ultimate sophistication" — Leonardo da Vinci

![leonardo da vinci simplicity quote](https://junjieblob.blob.core.windows.net/images/leonardo-da-vinci-simplicity-quote.png)

Rule of thumb:
- Only put **primary points** on slides — the key takeaways, short and memorable
- Save **secondary things** (background, rationale) for your verbal delivery

## Tip 3: Assume Lowest Common Language

Never assume your audience knows what you know:
- Assume **zero context** going in
- Spell out team-specific terminology
- Avoid project-specific acronyms without defining them first

## Tip 4: Follow Presentation Etiquette

1. Dial in 3–5 minutes early — no technical fumbling when it's your turn
2. Use a "holding slide" as your first slide (e.g., "We'll start at 9:05 sharp — feel free to do the cat-cow pose in the meantime")
3. Engage intentionally — make eye contact, ask questions, don't just read slides

## Tip 5: Rehearse Out Loud

Harvard Business Review: *"Most business professionals flip through their slides silently to prepare. Storytellers rehearse — out loud."*

Sounds obvious but most people skip this. Saying words out loud is completely different from reading them in your head. Do it at least once before the real thing.
