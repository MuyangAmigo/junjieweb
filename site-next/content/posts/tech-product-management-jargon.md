# Tech/Product Management Jargon — The Chinese Edition

A collection of buzzwords and 行话 that you hear constantly in Chinese tech companies. Half of these are genuinely useful frameworks. The other half are just... vibes.

![abstract digital workspace](https://junjieblob.blob.core.windows.net/images/abstract-digital-workspace.png)

## The Full List

落地、赋能、共创、分发、支撑、抓手、体感、感知、方法论、融合、调性、心智、拆解、集成、打法、解法、沉淀、对标、拉齐、聚焦、打通、打透、吃透、颗粒度、迁移、分层、漏斗、闭环、战役、组合拳、体系、赛道、兼容、包装、场景、商业布局、生态、打磨、探索、提炼、玩法、反哺、去中心化、勾兑、定性定量、细分、价值、响应速度、迭代、垂直、路径、场景、关键因子、输出、格局、结果导向、击穿心智、下钻、点线面、认知、强化心智、协同、纽带、连接、串联、价值转化、痛点、话术、颗粒度。

## A Note on Usage

Some of these (like 闭环, 颗粒度, 迭代, 漏斗) are actually useful and precise. Others (like 抓手, 赋能, 打透) get thrown around so much they've become almost meaningless. The trick is knowing which is which — and using the real ones with intention rather than just stacking jargon to sound credible.
