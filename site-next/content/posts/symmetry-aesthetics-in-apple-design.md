# Symmetry and Aesthetics in Apple's Design Language

Notes on Apple's design approach — specifically how they use symmetry, whitespace, and layout structure. Useful reference for product and UI design work.

## Core Principle: Centered Symmetry

Apple heavily uses centered, symmetrical layouts across their product pages. Everything reads as clean, natural, and balanced.

## Product Page Design

- 模块感强 — Strong sense of modular sections
- Modules are separated by either color contrast or whitespace — no heavy borders or dividers
- The overall effect is "simple and effortless"

## Typography and Image Layout

- Everything centers — from top to bottom, fully center-aligned
- Layout patterns: top-text-bottom-image, left-text-right-image, left-image-right-text
- Equal whitespace on both sides
- Image and text widths stay consistent across the page

## The Triangle Layout Trick

When two content blocks appear one after another on a long page, they're arranged in a subtle **triangle structure**.

![figma triangle layout diagram](/images/figma-triangle-layout-diagram.png)

![figma triangle layout tutorial](/images/figma-triangle-layout-tutorial.png)

**Why it works:**
- Creates guided reading — pulls the eye downward naturally
- Best for detail pages and long-form content
- Prevents the "wall of content" fatigue that makes people stop scrolling
- The triangular composition keeps things visually interesting without being distracting
